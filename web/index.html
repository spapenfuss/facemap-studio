    How to read the map (drawing order)
    1. 1st Midline, from forehead to nose tip.
    2. 2nd Level line across both brows at the top of the arch.
    3. 3rd Nostril edge through the outer eye corner: where each tail ends.
    4. 4th Inner V: the gap between the brow heads - keep it clear.
    5. 5th Start lines: each brow begins at the bridge of the nose, just inside the inner eye corner.
    6. 6th Arch lines: the peak sits on this vertical (nostril → pupil, dashed).
    7. 7th Tail lines: the tail ends level with the bottom of the brow head.
    8. 8th / 9th Upper and lower brow lines: the shape to trace.
    9. Bottom line (dashed): one level line under both brows - the underside of each brow should sit on it, so the bottoms match.
