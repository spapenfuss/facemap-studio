Color Analysis

Discover which colors make your skin look fresh and your eyes bright. This is the heart of OnFleek: your season also tunes your makeup colors.

What to do

  1. Start your live camera (or upload a photo if you prefer). Allow camera access when your browser asks.
  2. Face a window and sit back so your shoulders show. Natural daylight is best.
  3. Watch the colors appear under your face one by one. Pause, go back, or skip any time.
  4. Vote for the color in each pair that looks better on you. Then see your season.

For the most reliable result

  • Even daylight, no colored walls or warm lamps behind the screen.
  • No makeup if you can, hair pulled back, and a plain background.
  • Look straight at the camera with a relaxed face.

Check your lighting

Before we compare colors, we read your skin, hair and eye color from the same spots every time.

How are these estimated?

Your live video is never altered, so a fabric cannot literally change it, and these bars are not live readings of your skin. They are a model: they compare each color with the undertone, depth, clarity and contrast measured when you started (harmony, brightness, contrast fit: higher is better; dullness, yellow cast, redness, shadows: lower is better). Your own eyes decide the vote.

Which looked better on you?

Color analysis is guidance, not science: a single photograph cannot reliably establish your true undertone or season. Lighting, camera processing and makeup all affect it.
